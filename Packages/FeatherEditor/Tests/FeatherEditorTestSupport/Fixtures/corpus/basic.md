# Weather station

A small **outdoor** station, *logging* every ~~ten~~ five minutes.
Check the bus with `i2cdetect -y 1` and follow the [wiring guide](https://learn.example/bme280).

## Parts

- Pi Zero
- BME280
  - breakout
    - shield

### Steps

1. Power off.
2. Wire SDA.
10. Reboot.
